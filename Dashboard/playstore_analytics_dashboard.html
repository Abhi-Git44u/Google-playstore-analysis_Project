Key insights
🎮
Games dominate installs
Despite not being the most numerous category, Games lead with 13.9B installs — nearly 2x Communication in second place.
💸
Paid apps rate higher
Paid apps average 4.26★ vs 4.17★ for free apps. Quality filtering — users only pay for genuinely good apps.
😊
Mostly positive sentiment
64.1% of reviews are positive (polarity +0.37). Only 22.1% negative, suggesting strong overall user satisfaction.
👨‍👩‍👧
Family apps lead in count
Family category has 1,832 apps — nearly 2x Games (959). The Android market is strongly family-oriented by volume.
🌐
Everyone-rated dominates
81.8% of apps are rated "Everyone" — the market heavily prioritizes broad accessibility over niche or mature content.
📊
Ratings skew high
70.5% of apps have a 4★ rating or above. Median rating is 4.3★, showing the Play Store's strong quality floor.
